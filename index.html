OUR COMPANY

Experience built through recycling and reprocessing.

Jai Maruthi Polymers was established in 2009 as a basic plastic scrap sorting and recycling company.

Building on its knowledge and experience in plastics recycling and reprocessing, the company expanded into manufacturing and trading recycled and reprocessed polypropylene granules, regrinds and engineering polymers.

The company has a functional core team with expertise in technical, production and quality management and presently processes approximately 3600 MT of polymers annually.

MISSION

To achieve excellence in recycling and reprocessing of the plastic industry by benefiting the customer through property modification and value addition.
